# To Christians of the Third Millennium II

## The Architecture of Subjecthood

**To Christians of the Third Millennium II** is the second volume of the philosophical-theological project *To Christians of the Third Millennium (CTM)*.

This volume explores the architecture of human subjecthood through four interconnected studies:

- Genesis of Subjecthood
- Quality of Ontology
- Architecture of Subjecthood
- Eschatological Subjecthood

The original language of the work is Russian.

The English edition serves as the primary international edition.
